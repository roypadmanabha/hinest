1. Overview & Applicability

The Tripura Building Rules, 2017 (updated up to the 4th Amendment, 17.01.2025) provide the complete regulatory framework followed by all Urban Local Bodies (ULBs) in Tripura — including the Agartala Municipal Corporation (AMC) — for reviewing and approving building plans.

Who It Applies To

Anyone erecting, re-erecting, adding to, or altering a building inside a Tripura ULB area — residential, commercial, institutional, or industrial.

What You'll Need

A sanctioned site plan (Form A), a building plan (Form B) prepared by empanelled architects/engineers/planners, tax clearance, and applicable fees.

Why It Matters

Building outside these limits leads to plan rejection, stop-work orders, or demolition of unauthorised portions at the owner's cost.

2. Step-by-Step Building Plan Approval Process

Here is the exact journey a building plan undergoes in Agartala (AMC), from plot verification to obtaining your occupancy certificate:

1

Get your site approved first (Form A)

Before applying for a building plan, your plot needs a sanctioned site plan. The ULB verifies plot access road width, drainage, neighboring structures, and site boundary conditions.

2

Submit the building plan (Form B)

Must be prepared by the required combination of Architect, Structural Engineer, Geo-technical Engineer, or Licensed Building Planner along with tax clearance, ownership proof, and FAR calculations.

3

ULB reviews within 30 days

The ULB may request missing details within 15 working days. Once complete, it must sanction, refuse, or conditionally sanction within 30 days. If unaddressed after a follow-up representation for another 30 days, the plan is deemed approved by default.

4

Pay the fee & receive building permit

Building permits are issued upon fee payment. Sanctioned plans remain valid for 3 years, renewable for 2 years (extending up to 5 years if partly built with partial occupancy).

5

Notice before work (Form E) & Plinth notice (Form F)

Inform the ULB in writing (Form E) at least 7 days before construction begins, and again (Form F) when construction reaches plinth level for inspection.

6

Completion notice (Form G) & Occupancy Certificate

Submit completion notice once work ends. No building may be legally occupied until full or partial occupancy certificate is issued.

Appeal Process: If your plan is refused, you can appeal directly to the Local Body for reconsideration without starting from scratch.

3. Technical Personnel Required by Building Height

The taller or more complex your proposed building, the higher the qualification required for signing professionals:

Building Height / Type Professionals Required to Sign
Up to 4.5 metres Licensed Building Planner (min. diploma holder with 2+ years experience)
Above 4.5 m up to 8.0 m (or basements/deep foundations) Degree-holder Building Planner and/or Architect + Structural Engineer
Above 8.0 m up to 14.0 m (or piling/deep foundation/basement) Building Planner + Architect + Structural Engineer + Geo-technical Engineer
Above 14.0 m (Tall Building) Same team as above + State Government approval via ULB and Technical Appraisal Committee (TAC)
Empanelment Mandatory: All Structural Engineers, Geo-technical Engineers, and Licensed Building Planners must be empanelled with the ULB to sign building plans.

4. Maximum Permissible Ground Coverage

Ground coverage represents the maximum percentage of your total plot area that the building footprint is permitted to occupy for a single building on a plot.

Plot Size Building Use Type Max Ground Coverage
Up to 200 sq. mtr Residential 70%
Other than residential 50%
Above 200 sq. mtr All building types 60%

* For plots containing more than one building, coverage is evaluated under multi-building rules by the ULB.

5. Permissible Building Height & Floor Area Ratio (FAR)

Maximum building height is directly governed by the width of the access road or passage in front of your plot:

Width of Means of Access (Road Width) Maximum Building Height Allowed
1.80 m – 2.40 m Up to 8.00 m
Above 2.40 m – 5.00 m Up to 10.00 m
Above 5.00 m – 8.00 m Up to 12.50 m
Above 8.00 m – 15.00 m Up to 14.50 m
Above 15.00 m Tall Building — Special State Govt & TAC rules apply
Height Measurement: Height is measured from the road's centre-line level to the highest structural point. Excluded from height: stair covers (≤2.40m), lift machine rooms, roof tanks (≤1.80m), chimneys, and parapets (≤1.50m) provided they cover ≤ 1/3 of roof area.

Floor Area Ratio (FAR) for Buildings >14.5 m Height:

Access Road Width Residential (Commercial Zone) Residential (Other Zone) Institutional / Business / Others
Above 14.5 m – 20.00 m 2.50 2.75 2.50
Above 20.00 m 2.75 3.00 2.75

* Excluded from FAR calculation: Covered car parking (calculated at 20 sqm per car), stair covers, lift machine rooms, roof water tanks, and chimneys.

6. Front, Rear & Side Open Spaces (Setbacks)

Minimum open spaces required around the building at ground level for light, ventilation, and emergency access:

Front Open Space

    Residential: 1.80 m Assembly / Inst. / Educational / Club: 3.00 m Commercial (built-up >100 sqm): 4.00 m Industrial / Wholesale / Storage: 5.00 m Small plots (≤165 sqm, ≤8m ht): 1.20 m

* Mixed-use buildings adopt the highest front setback among the building's proposed uses.

Rear Open Space

    Up to 8.00 m height: 1.00 m Above 8.00 – 10.00 m: 1.20 m Above 10.00 – 12.50 m: 2.00 m Above 12.50 – 14.50 m: 2.50 m

Side Open Space

    Up to 8.00 m (G+1): 1.00 m each side Above 8.00 – 14.50 m: 1.20 m each side Facing existing door/window: 1.80 m min. Industrial / Storage: 3.50 m min.

* Plots deeper than 24 m require a 4.0 m passage full depth on one side.

7. Parking Space Requirements

Standard car parking dimension is 2.40 m × 4.50 m; truck/bus space is 3.75 m × 10.00 m (excluding driveways and circulation aisles):

Building Occupancy Use Covered Area Threshold Mandatory Parking Required
Residential Up to 70 sqm: None 1 car space / additional 100 sqm
Commercial / Business Up to 50 sqm: None 1 car space / additional 75 sqm
Restaurants, Bars, Clubs — 1 car space / 100 sqm
Hotels & Lodges — 1 car space / 200 sqm
Theatres, Auditoria, Cinemas — 1 car space / 100 sqm
Hospitals & Healthcare — 1 car space / 100 sqm
Educational Institutions — 1 car / 400 sqm + 1 bus / 1000 sqm
Industrial & Storage — 1 car / 400 sqm + 1 truck / 1000 sqm
Parking Notes: Residential buildings up to 14 m height do not require direct driveway access to every individual car space. Access roads narrower than 2.40 m may be granted parking waivers. Stilt parking must be constructed with shear walls or cross-bracing.

8. Minimum Room Sizes & Interior Heights

Every internal room and utility space must meet mandatory floor area and clear height minimums:

Room / Space Type Minimum Floor Area Minimum Clear Height
Habitable Single Room 9.50 sqm (min width 2.40 m) 2.75 m
Additional Habitable Rooms 7.50 sqm (min width 2.10 m) 2.75 m
Kitchen 4.50 sqm (min width 1.80 m; 9.5 sqm if with dining) 2.50 m
Pantry 3.00 sqm (min width 1.40 m) —
Bathroom 1.80 sqm (min width 1.20 m; 2.6 sqm with WC) 2.20 m
Water Closet (WC) 1.10 sqm (min width 0.90 m) —
Store Room 1.50 sqm 2.10 m
Car Garage 2.50 m × 5.00 m 2.10 m
Retail Shop — 2.30 m

Loft & Mezzanine

Lofts: Max 25% area of non-habitable rooms (100% over corridors), min 2.10m clear height. Mezzanine: Max 25% ground floor covered area, ground-to-first floor only, no kitchen use.

Boundary Wall

Max 2.0 m height above road center-line, solid portion capped at 1.5 m. Foundations must not intrude onto adjacent plots.

Parapet Wall

Mandatory on roof terraces and balcony edges. Capped at 1.5 m (excess height counts toward overall building height).

9. Fire Safety & Exit Regulations

Mandatory for buildings taller than 12.50 m or exceeding 500 sqm covered area — requires formal NOC approval from the Director of Fire Services:

Exit Requirements

    Exits must remain unobstructed, lit, and clearly marked. Lifts and escalators never qualify as fire exits. Basements require at least 2 independent fire exits.

Maximum Exit Travel Distance

    Residential/Educational/Institutional: 22.5 m max Assembly/Commercial/Business/Industrial: 30.0 m max Travel limits increase by 50% for fully sprinklered floors.
Staircase Rules: Buildings over 14.0 m height require at least 2 staircases (one positioned on an external wall). Minimum stair width ranges from 0.90 m up to 2.00 m+ for high-occupancy assembly buildings. All electrical installations follow the National Electrical Code.

10. Structural Design & Safety Standards

All structural designs and retrofitting must strictly comply with National Building Code (NBC) structural provisions and relevant Indian Standards (IS codes):

  • Designs must be performed and signed exclusively by a ULB-empanelled Structural Engineer.
  • Complies with IS codes for seismic resistance, foundations, concrete, steel, masonry, and timber.
  • Proof-checking of structural calculations by competent authorities for designated high-risk or tall structures.
  • Mandatory soil testing and geotechnical reports required prior to foundation approval.
  • Alternative building materials permitted only upon official laboratory certification of structural equivalence.

11. Basement Construction Rules

Where basements are permitted by the ULB, the following strict regulations apply:

Allowed Uses

    Car parking & vehicle storage AC plant rooms, generators, electrical panels Storage for non-combustible goods Bank strong rooms, libraries, dark rooms Air-conditioned retail, office, or assembly space

Prohibited Uses & Specs

    No residential occupancy of any kind No kitchen, bathroom, or WC (unless sewer clear) Minimum 2.4 m floor-to-ceiling clear height Must have 2 ramps (≥3.5m wide, max slope 1:6) + separate stairs Damp-proofing & mechanical ventilation (1 fan/50sqm)

12. Tall Building Rules (Exceeding 14.5m)

Buildings taller than 14.50 m are treated as special cases requiring formal State Government clearance and Technical Appraisal Committee (TAC) sanction:

Key Conditions for Tall Buildings:
  • Not permitted on streets narrower than 15.00 m width.
  • Front setback: 20% of building height or 6.0 m (whichever is less).
  • Rear setback: 5.0 m min (up to 18m ht), 20% ht beyond 18m.
  • Side setback: 3.5 m min (up to 18m ht), 20% ht beyond 18m.
  • Mechanical ventilation mandatory for all interior shafts.

13. Accessibility (Barrier-Free Provisions)

Applies to public-use buildings (educational, commercial, institutional, assembly, group housing) on plots larger than 2,000 sqm:

Mandatory Features

    Barrier-free non-slip access pathway from plot entrance to building lobby. Reserved accessible parking spaces near main entrances. Ramp entrance alongside steps with handrails on both sides. Wheelchair-accessible doors, corridors, and at least 1 accessible toilet on ground floor (or every floor with a lift).

Signage & Refuge Areas

    Wheelchair-sized lifts with auditory & Braille indicators. Tactile floor guidance paths for visually impaired visitors. Designated fire refuge areas near fireproof stairwells on every floor. Complies with Govt. of India Harmonized Guidelines.

14. Green Building & Sustainability Requirements

Rainwater Harvesting

Mandatory for residential plots ≥200 sqm plinth area, all group housing/public buildings, and commercial/industrial plots ≥50 sqm. Full rainwater proposal required at plan submission.

Solar & Energy

Rooftop Solar mandatory for plots ≥500 sqm (generating 5% connected load or 20W/sqft roof). Solar Water Heating mandatory for hotels, hospitals, lodges, homes >150 sqm plinth.

STP & Building Insurance: Buildings >10,000 sqm (or ≥10,000 L/day wastewater) must install on-site Sewage Treatment Plants (STP). Building insurance against fire and natural disasters is mandatory for residential buildings >300 sqm / 3+ storeys and non-residential buildings of 2+ storeys.

15. Occupancy Certificate Regulations

  • Submit completion notice (Form G) upon construction finish; ULB inspects within 7 working days.
  • Occupancy certificate issued within 10 working days of inspection if compliant with sanctioned plan.
  • Unlawful Occupation: No building may be legally occupied or used without a valid occupancy certificate.
  • Partial occupancy certificates permitted for completed sections with an indemnity bond.
  • Water and sewerage connections will not be released by authorities prior to occupancy certificate issuance.

16. Deviations, Violations & Demolition Orders

Internal Deviations

Minor internal modifications that do not violate rules or increase covered area are permissible by giving 15 days written notice with architect certification before work.

Major Additions

Any work exceeding sanctioned covered area requires a revised plan and fresh sanction prior to construction. Post-construction regularization is not permitted.

Unauthorised Construction: Construction without sanction or departing from approved plans is subject to demolition orders. If the owner fails to demolish within notice period, the ULB executes demolition and recovers total costs from the owner.
